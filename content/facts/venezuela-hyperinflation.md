Currency debasement isn't just a theory — it's happened repeatedly, and
recently. In Venezuela, the Bolivar traded at about 2 to the US dollar in
2009. By 2019, it took roughly 250,000 Bolivars to buy that same dollar,
as the government printed money to cover its spending. Savings held in
Bolivars were wiped out.

This is what "trust the central bank not to debase the currency" actually
risks in practice: not an abstract inconvenience, but watching a lifetime
of savings evaporate within a decade, without the saver doing anything
wrong at all.

**Analogy:** Imagine saving your paycheck under your mattress and coming
back ten years later to find it's worth about one candy bar.
