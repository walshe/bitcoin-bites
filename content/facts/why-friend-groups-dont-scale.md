A shared ledger kept by a small group of friends works because
everyone has a social reason not to cheat — lying to your friends has
real consequences. But that breaks down as the network grows. Once
the group is large enough that people don't know or trust each other
personally, there's a real incentive to quietly credit yourself extra
money in your own copy of the ledger, with no social pressure
stopping anyone from trying.

This is the real problem Bitcoin has to solve: how do you keep a
shared ledger honest among total strangers, at global scale, with no
one in charge and no reputations at stake?

**Analogy:** Splitting a dinner bill with five close friends works on the
honor system; splitting it with five thousand strangers who'll never
see each other again does not.
