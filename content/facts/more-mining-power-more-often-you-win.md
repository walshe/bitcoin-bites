Since every attempt at the mining lottery is an independent random
guess, controlling more of the network's total hashing power just
means you get to buy proportionally more lottery tickets, not a
guaranteed win. Control 10% of everyone's combined computing power
(the network's "hash rate") and you'll win roughly 10% of blocks over
time — but on any single block, luck can still go the other way for
hours or even days.

**Analogy:** Like buying 10% of the raffle tickets in the box — you'll
win about a tenth of the time on average, but any individual drawing
could still go to someone else entirely.
