Bitcoin best practice is to use a brand new address for every payment
you receive, which sounds like it should mean backing up thousands
of separate private keys. A pair of improvements fixed that: one
random "seed" number can deterministically regenerate an entire tree
of key pairs (so backing up the seed backs up every address that will
ever come from it), and that seed can itself be represented as a short
sequence of ordinary words instead of a string of digits — something
like "witch collapse practice feed shame open despair creek road
again ice least."

That plain-language format means your entire net worth in Bitcoin can
be written on a slip of paper, locked in a safe, or simply memorized —
letting someone walk across a border out of a collapsing economy with
their savings carried entirely in their head, nothing to search or seize.

**Analogy:** Like being handed a master key that regrows into every
lock you'll ever own, small enough to memorize as a short poem instead
of carrying a heavy keyring.
