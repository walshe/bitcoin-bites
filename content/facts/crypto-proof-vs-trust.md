Traditional finance runs on claims you have to take on faith: a bank tells
you "you have $10 in your account," and you trust their word (and their
database). Bitcoin replaces that faith with cryptographic math — a way of
proving facts (like "I own this coin" or "I signed this transaction") that
anyone can check for themselves, without trusting the person making the
claim at all.

This single substitution — proof instead of trust — is the core trick
behind almost everything else Bitcoin does: verifying ownership, preventing
forgery, and securing the network, all without a referee.

**Analogy:** A tamper-proof, verifiable receipt versus someone simply
telling you "trust me, I paid." One can be checked by anyone; the other can
only be believed.
