Bitcoin is peer-to-peer electronic cash — digital money that moves between
people or computers with no trusted middleman (like a bank) in between, and
whose issuance isn't under any single party's control.

Before Bitcoin, digital payments always needed a middleman. Physical cash
works differently: when you hand someone a $20 bill, they just check it with
their own eyes and fingers — no bank required. But as payments went digital,
we lost that: a credit card network, PayPal, or WeChat now has to approve and
verify every payment, because digital bits (unlike physical cash) need
someone trusted to control their transfer. Bitcoin brings the "just check it
yourself" property of cash back to digital money.

**Analogy:** Like emailing someone a photo — except that the moment it
arrives, your copy is gone. Everything else you send digitally is
really a copy; this is the first kind that actually leaves your hands.
