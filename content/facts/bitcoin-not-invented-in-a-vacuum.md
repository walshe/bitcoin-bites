Bitcoin didn't spring from nowhere. In the original whitepaper, Satoshi
cited earlier cypherpunk work directly, including Wei Dai's "b-money" and
Adam Back's "Hashcash" — attempts at digital cash and proof systems dating
back to the 1990s that never quite became real, usable money.

What made Bitcoin different wasn't a single new idea; it was combining
these existing pieces in a way simple enough to actually work, creating the
first truly decentralized system for issuing and transferring digital
money — decentralized meaning no one person or company controls it.

**Analogy:** Like the Wright brothers' plane — not the first attempt at
flight, and not built from entirely new physics, but the first one that
actually stayed in the air.
