Every prior form of money — from seashells to gold to today's dollars — could
eventually be debased by whoever controlled its supply: a king who clipped
coins, a central bank that printed more. Bitcoin is the first monetary system
in history where the total supply (21 million coins) is fixed by code that
every participant can verify, and that no person, company, or government can
increase — not even Bitcoin's own creator.

**Analogy:** Imagine a lottery that pays out a shrinking jackpot every ten
minutes, halving roughly every four years, until the last fractional coin is
handed out around the year 2140. No one runs the lottery — the schedule was
fixed at the start and enforced by thousands of independent computers that
would all have to agree, and fail to notice, for it to ever change.
