Mining is pure chance: a small miner could burn hundreds of dollars
of electricity for days without ever finding a winning hash, since
having a small slice of the network's hash rate only sets your average
odds, not your actual luck on any given day. Mining pools exist to
smooth that out. Many miners combine their hash power so the pool
as a whole wins blocks regularly, then splits each reward among
everyone proportional to how much hash power they actually
contributed — in exchange for a small cut for coordinating it.

This lets even a single home computer contributing a tiny sliver of
hash rate earn a small, steady, predictable payout instead of gambling
for an all-or-nothing block reward that might never come.

**Analogy:** Like a group of coworkers pooling their lottery tickets and
splitting any winnings by how many tickets each person chipped in —
nobody waits alone for their one ticket to hit.
